IMPORTANT — you've set replicaCount to {{ .Values.replicaCount }}:
Wardline's budget enforcement (rate limiting) and dashboard live-activity
view are both per-process, in-memory state — NOT shared across replicas.
With {{ .Values.replicaCount }} replicas, each pod enforces its own
independent rate limit and shows its own independent live audit view;
a caller's requests may be load-balanced across replicas, effectively
multiplying their real rate limit by {{ .Values.replicaCount }}. This is
not a bug in this chart — it's the current, documented behavior of
Wardline's in-memory budget limiter and dashboard ring buffer. A shared
(e.g. Postgres- or Redis-backed) budget store is real future work, not
something this chart can paper over.
{{- end }}
