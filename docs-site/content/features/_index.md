---
title: "Features"
---

One page per capability: what problem it solves, the minimal config to
turn it on, and its known, honestly-documented limitations. The v0.1
baseline is always on; most other capabilities are opt-in behind a
`features` flag in `wardline.yaml`. Compliance evidence export and the
policy-pack marketplace are the exceptions — both are always-available
CLI subcommands, not gated by any flag.
